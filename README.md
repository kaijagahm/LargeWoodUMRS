# LargeWoodUMRS

**Institution:** University of Wisconsin, La Crosse; USGS

**Publication:** Van Appledorn, Molly, KathiJo Jankowski, Kaija Gahm, et al. 2024. “The Where and Why of Large Wood Occurrence in the Upper Mississippi and Illinois Rivers.” Earth Surface Processes and Landforms 49 (11): 3383–98. https://doi.org/10.1002/esp.5911.

## Description

Code related to the UWL-REU project on Large Wood, specifically on the distribution of large wood in space and time, and relationship with environmental variables.

Analysis conducted during Summer 2018 REU internship, La Crosse, Wisconsin.
